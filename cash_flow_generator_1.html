How this cash flow statement is prepared — Ind AS 7 in brief

Objective & scope (paras 1–14)

Ind AS 7 requires an entity to present a Statement of Cash Flows classifying cash flows during the period into Operating, Investing, and Financing activities. Cash equivalents are short-term, highly liquid investments readily convertible to known amounts of cash, subject to insignificant risk of value change (para 6–9) — typically original maturity of three months or less.

Indirect method (para 18(b), used by this tool)

  • Start from Profit Before Tax.
  • Add back non-cash items embedded in profit: depreciation & amortisation, finance costs, impairments, fair-value movements, unrealised (profit)/loss on disposals, and similar adjustments — this gives Operating Profit before Working Capital Changes.
  • Adjust for movements in working-capital items (inventories, receivables, payables, provisions, other operating assets/liabilities) to arrive at Cash Generated from Operations.
  • Deduct income tax paid to arrive at Net Cash from Operating Activities.

Investing & Financing activities (paras 16–17)

Investing activities cover acquisition/disposal of long-term assets and other investments not classified as cash equivalents (PPE, intangibles, investment property, investments, business combinations under Ind AS 103). Financing activities cover changes in the size/composition of contributed equity and borrowings (share issues, borrowings drawn/repaid, lease repayments, dividends paid).

Classification choices applied automatically by this tool

  • Interest paid → Financing (para 31)
  • Interest & dividend received → Investing (para 31)
  • Dividends paid (to owners and, in consolidated statements, to NCI) → Financing (para 34)
  • Income tax paid → Operating, unless specifically identifiable with investing/financing activity (para 35)
  • Obtaining/losing control of a subsidiary → Investing, presented net of cash & cash equivalents acquired/disposed (paras 39–42, read with Ind AS 103)
  • Changes in ownership interest with no loss of control → Financing, presented gross (para 42A)
  • Non-cash investing/financing transactions (bonus issues, share-swap mergers/demergers, debt-to-equity conversion, new right-of-use assets at lease inception) → excluded from the statement entirely and disclosed separately as a memorandum (para 43)
  • Bank balances not meeting the definition of a cash equivalent (e.g. deposits with original maturity beyond three months, margin money, earmarked balances) → excluded from cash & cash equivalents and separately disclosed (para 45)

Why the reconciliation check matters

Opening cash + net change from all three activities + FX effect (consolidated only) must equal closing cash per the Balance Sheet. A variance almost always means an item was missed, double-counted, or wrongly classified as non-cash when it should flow through the statement (or vice versa) — see the reconciliation box on the Cash Flow Statement tab for tool-generated hints specific to your figures.

Prepared for reference using figures you provide; not a substitute for professional judgement. Interest paid, interest and dividend received, dividends paid, and bank overdrafts repayable on demand are classified according to the policy you set in Statement Setup, and the policy actually applied is printed with every statement (Ind AS 7 paras 8, 31–34). Taxes on income are treated as Operating unless specifically identifiable with an investing or financing activity (para 35); non-cash transactions (e.g. bonus shares, conversion of debt to equity, share-swap mergers/demergers, new right-of-use assets at lease inception) are excluded from the statement (para 43) and disclosed separately. Working-capital movements automatically use Inventories, Trade Receivables/Payables (current + non-current), Loans & Advances (current + non-current), Contract Assets and Contract Liabilities, Other Current/Non-current Assets & Liabilities (non-financial), Provisions, and any Balance Sheet line you have added yourself and classified as an operating asset or operating liability; the "Other Financial Assets/Liabilities" lines are treated as investing/financing in nature and are excluded from the automatic working-capital calculation — their cash effect is expected to be captured through the relevant Additional Input (e.g. current maturities of long-term debt through "Repayment of Long-term Borrowings", accrued interest through "Interest Paid/Received", unpaid dividends through "Dividend Paid").
